# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- Assuming the other person is reasonable and asking before concluding otherwise
- Giving and accepting technical criticism about the work, not the author
- Saying plainly when you were wrong, and moving on without ceremony
- Remembering that most contributors here are engineers writing in a second language.
  Judge the substance, not the phrasing.

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to **krzysztof.augiewicz@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and the reporter's
identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer will respond in a way proportionate to what happened: usually a private word,
sometimes a public correction with an apology requested, and for sustained or serious
behaviour a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
