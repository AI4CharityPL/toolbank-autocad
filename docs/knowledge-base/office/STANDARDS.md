# Office — legal & code standards

> **Reference standards:** Rozp. MPiPS z 26.09.1997 w sprawie ogólnych przepisów BHP (tekst jednolity
> **Dz.U. 2003 nr 169 poz. 1650**) · WT 2024 (Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1225) · Ustawa z 24.08.1991 o
> ochronie ppoż. (kategoria ZL III)

Unlike hospital (Rozp. MZ 2019 governs room requirements directly) and residential (WT
Rozdział 7 governs apartments directly), office workstation/room requirements in Polish law come
from **workplace health-and-safety regulation (BHP)**, not the building-conditions regulation
(WT) — WT still governs the building envelope/fire class, but per-person floor area and room
height for office work come from Rozp. MPiPS. This is itself a useful finding: don't assume
every typology's checkable numbers live in the same source act.

## Legal-hierarchy table

| Skrót | Pełna nazwa | Rola w projekcie |
|---|---|---|
| **Rozp. MPiPS BHP** | Rozp. Ministra Pracy i Polityki Socjalnej z 26.09.1997 w sprawie ogólnych przepisów bezpieczeństwa i higieny pracy (tj. **Dz.U. 2003 nr 169 poz. 1650**) | powierzchnia i wysokość pomieszczeń pracy stałej — §19, §20 |
| **WT 2024** | Rozp. MI z 12.04.2002 (tj. **Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1225**) | budynek jako całość: klasa odporności ogniowej, ewakuacja, dostępność |
| **Ust. ppoż.** | Ustawa z 24.08.1991 (t.j. Dz.U. 2024) | klasyfikacja ZL, wymogi instalacji ppoż. |

## Key checkable numbers

| Requirement | Value | Citation | Confidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Floor area per employee (permanent workroom) | **≥ 2 m²** free floor (not occupied by equipment/furniture) | Rozp. MPiPS BHP § 19 ust. 2 | **Confirmed** — quoted verbatim from the regulation this session |
| Air volume per employee | **≥ 13 m³** | Rozp. MPiPS BHP § 19 ust. 2 | **Confirmed** — same paragraph |
| Room height, no harmful factors | **≥ 3 m** clear | Rozp. MPiPS BHP § 20 ust. 1 pkt 1 | **Confirmed** — quoted verbatim |
| Room height, harmful factors present | **≥ 3.3 m** clear | Rozp. MPiPS BHP § 20 ust. 1 pkt 2 | **Confirmed** |
| Reduced height with AC | **≥ 2.5 m**, if ≤4 employees AND ≥15 m³/employee, sanitary-inspector approval required | Rozp. MPiPS BHP § 20 ust. 2-3 | **Confirmed** |
| Fire category | **ZL III** (public-use building not otherwise classified ZL I/ZL II) — a room for >50 non-permanent occupants (e.g. large conference room) may need to be classified ZL I within the building | WT / Ust. ppoż. classification practice, exact § sub-point not independently pinned this session | **Probable** — the ZL III catch-all definition and the >50-occupant carve-out are confirmed by multiple sources; the precise WT §209 sub-paragraph number was not re-verified against primary text (see hospital's §209 ust. 1 pkt 2 for the analogous ZL II citation pattern) |
| One-way passage width to a workstation | **≥ 0.75 m** | reported, not independently re-verified this session | **Probable** |
| Two-way passage width | **≥ 0.9 m** | reported, not independently re-verified this session | **Probable** |

## Sourcing note

BHP §19/§20 figures were fetched and quoted directly from a legal-text aggregator
(lexlege.pl / przepisy.gofin.pl) this session, not from isap.sejm.gov.pl directly (same access
issue as the residential typology — CAPTCHA/undecodable PDF). The ZL III classification and
passage-width figures are corroborated by multiple secondary sources but not independently
re-verified against primary text; flagged accordingly, matching the residential typology's
honesty standard rather than presenting them with false certainty.
